FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Is this an official government tool?

No. This is an independent estimate built by Gov iPhone. Official eligibility is determined by USAC's National Verifier (Oregon and Texas use state processes).

Which programs count for the program route?

Medicaid, SNAP/EBT, Supplemental Security Income (SSI), Federal Public Housing Assistance, Veterans Pension or Survivors Benefit, and qualifying Tribal programs.

How much is the Lifeline discount in 2026?

Up to $9.25 per month off phone or broadband service, or up to $34.25 per month for households on qualifying Tribal lands.

Can I get more than one Lifeline benefit?

No. Only one Lifeline benefit is allowed per household, whether it is used for phone or broadband service.

What do I do if I likely qualify?

Apply through the National Verifier, then pick a participating carrier. You can compare carriers side by side at goviphone.com/plans.

Important: This checker gives an estimate only. It is not an official eligibility determination and not an application.
